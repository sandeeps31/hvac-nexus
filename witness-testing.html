What happens on submit: witnesses + signatures are cloned onto each eligible runsheet. Each runsheet computes its own outcome (signed_off if all tests pass, failed_pending_rework if any fail), generates defects per failed test, and (for equipment runsheets only) flips the Cx tracker stage 7 to "Y" on a pass. Already-locked and blocked runsheets are skipped — the rest are processed in sequence.
